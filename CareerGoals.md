## My Career Goal

One of the career goals I am currently working toward is improving my **organizational and project management skills**.

I believe these skills are important for a career in data science because I will be expected to structure datasets and organize code and project files. As a Data Science major, I have realized that being organized is important when balancing coursework, assignments, and other responsibilities.

## How I'm Working Toward This Goal

I am currently developing better organizational habits through the following strategies:

| Strategy | How I'm Applying It |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Daily To-Do Lists** | Tracking assignments and responsibilities every day |
| **Task Prioritization** | Completing the most important tasks first |
| **Two-Minute Rule** | Immediately completing tasks that take less than two minutes |
| **Planning Ahead** | Adding next-day planning to my daily to-do list |

One area I have struggled with is planning what I want to do the night before. I often forget and end up planning in the morning instead. To address this, I have started including next-day planning as a task on my to-do list.

Through these changes, I hope to become more efficient and consistent with my work.

## Future Career Interests

**Industry:** Finance  
**Potential Career:** Financial Data Analyst

In the future, I would like to pursue a career in finance, potentially working in financial data analysis.

I believe stronger organizational skills will help me succeed in this field because managing financial data requires accuracy, consistency, and attention to detail.

## Long-Term Development

By continuing to develop these skills throughout college, I hope to build organizational habits that I can maintain throughout my career.

Improving my organization now will help prepare me for the responsibilities and expectations of working professionally in data science.
