5. What Stood Out to Me
What surprised me most was how different the responsibilities were between the two finance-focused jobs. The first posting focused more on budgeting and accounting, and financial policies, while the second focused more on analysing data and improving financial performance. I was also surprised by how much both jobs emphasized communication and teamwork because I originally thought financial analysts mostly worked independently with numbers. Overall, this helped me understand how much variety there can be within financial careers.
